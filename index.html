THE FILMMAKER

STORIES WITH
A POINT OF VIEW.

Nazeem Fahman is a filmmaker, director, editor and visual storyteller based in Malaysia. His work moves across narrative film, documentary, branded content, television, photography and visual design.

He approaches every project from the story first, then builds the images, rhythm and atmosphere around what the audience should feel.
